# kuozai-opc

**kuozai-opc** is a modular digital platform designed specifically for **One Person Companies (OPCs)**. Whether you're a solopreneur, freelancer, or independent creator, this project helps you centralize your business presence, user management, and customer communication.

> *Currently in early ideation – contributions and feedback are welcome!*

---

## 🎯 Vision

Empower individuals running their own company with a cost‑effective, easy‑to‑deploy digital infrastructure. Instead of piecing together multiple services, `kuozai-opc` aims to provide the essential building blocks for a modern OPC.

---

## 🧩 Planned Modules

### 1. Official Website (OPC Portal)
- Introduce yourself and your company story  
- Showcase your services, products, or portfolio  
- Clean, responsive design suitable for any niche  

### 2. User Center Service
- User registration & authentication  
- Role‑based access control (RBAC)  
- Manage user profiles, preferences, and activity history  

### 3. Smart Customer Assistant
- AI‑powered chatbot to handle common inquiries  
- Seamless handoff to human (the OPC owner) when needed  
- Supports messaging channels (web, maybe Telegram/WhatsApp later)
